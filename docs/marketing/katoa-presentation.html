Share a link. Get paid. Keep it.

What it is

A page (profile + wishlists) you share. People send Bitcoin over Lightning to your wallet. Same idea as a tip jar or a subscribe button — Katoa’s cut is 0%, forever. No bank. No KYC. No week-long PayPal hold.

Who it’s for

Students, waitstaff, musicians, skaters, tutors — anyone with fans or regulars. Sign up with email or Google. Add your Lightning address. Supporters scan a QR and tap. Katoa never holds the money.

Why it feels real

Lightning is coffee-money speed in 195+ countries. Wishlists turn “I need rent / gear / a skate ramp” into a list with progress. Profiles look like a modern feed — without the platform owning you. Open source, so 0% is auditable.

Why you’d use it

The old internet taxes the work. ~20% here, $40/mo there, frozen banks everywhere. Investor: a creator economy not rented from Visa. 25-year-old: share a link, get paid, keep it.

Honest today: gifts use the creator’s saved Lightning address. Production invoices are still being wired. Subscribe / likes are labeled demo until settlement is live. Sample catalog is 11 creators — never “thousands.”
