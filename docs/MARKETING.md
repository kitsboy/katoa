## Positioning statement

**KATOA** is the privacy-first, **0% fee** platform for creators, makers, causes, and everyday people who want to give or receive support **directly** — without banks, platforms, or middlemen taking a cut.

It converts *“I want to help this person”* into an instant, borderless, private Bitcoin Lightning payment the recipient actually keeps.

---

## The problem (make it visceral)

Traditional platforms charge **5–20%** plus payment processing. Most require:

- A bank account  
- KYC / government ID  
- Living in a “supported” country (~10–50)  
- Waiting **7–30 days** for payout  
- Accepting censorship and deplatforming risk  

**The creator does the work. The platform owns the relationship and skims forever.**

For givers: you want to help someone directly. Instead you get surveillance checkout, hidden fees, and slow settlement.

**Emotional hook:** *“How much of your support actually reached them?”*

## Visual & design direction

Align all campaigns with [`DESIGN.md`](./DESIGN.md):
