## Core Positioning

**KATOA = Keep All That's Owed Always**

KATOA is the privacy-first, zero-fee Bitcoin Lightning platform for creators, makers, causes, and normal people who want to receive (or give) support directly — without banks, platforms, or middlemen taking a cut.

It turns "I want to help this person / project / dream" into an instant, borderless, private Bitcoin Lightning payment that the recipient actually keeps 100% of.

It is **not** another rent-seeking creator platform. It is infrastructure for a better creator economy and a better way to give: built on the hardest, most sovereign money ever created, with privacy tools (Nostr today, PYNYM + more tomorrow) so users stay in control.

**One-line hook (use everywhere)**:  
"Zero fees. Instant Bitcoin. 195+ countries. Creators keep 100%."

**Slightly longer (README hero style)**:  
"Zero-fee, privacy-first Bitcoin commerce. Protocol-level upgrade to creator monetization."

## The Problem (Make It Relatable)

Most platforms that help creators get paid take 5–20% + payment processing fees. Many require:
- A bank account
- KYC / ID verification
- Living in one of ~10 "supported" countries
- Waiting 7–30 days for payout
- Accepting censorship or deplatforming risk

Even "generous" ones still extract rent. The creator does the work; the platform owns the relationship and skims forever.

For givers: you want to support someone directly and privately. Instead you get funneled through surveillance-heavy rails, high fees, and slow settlement.

**Result**: Billions of dollars (and sats) lost to fees, friction, and gatekeepers every year. Many people simply can't participate.

---

## The KATOA Solution (Simple & Powerful)

- **0% platform fees — forever.** The creator receives every sat you send (minus only the tiny Lightning network fee, often <1 sat).
- **Instant settlement.** Lightning Network = seconds, not "payout on the 1st and 15th."
- **No bank, no KYC, no borders.** Works for people in 195+ countries. Bitcoin doesn't ask for permission.
- **True privacy foundations.** Sign in with Nostr (your keys, your identity). Publish wishlists to the open network. Future layers (PYNYM mixnet, BIP-47 PayNyms, Silent Payments) will make even the act of giving and receiving more private.
- **Simple for normal humans.** Beautiful UI, QR codes, Lightning addresses, one-click-ish flows. You don't need to be a Bitcoiner to use it — but it respects you if you are.
- **Multiple revenue models in one place**: one-off donations, wishlist items (with progress tracking and merchant links), subscriptions (future), crowdfunding goals, etc.
- **Open source (MIT)**. The code is public. No one can rug the rules or the data model against you.

**For the giver**: You get to send real value, instantly, privately, and know almost all of it reached the person you care about.

**For the creator**: You actually keep what you're owed. You own your audience relationship. You can live anywhere and still get paid by anyone.
