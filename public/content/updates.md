Saint-Martin presents a fascinating jurisdictional arbitrage opportunity for Bitcoin-forward entities. As a French collectivité, it has autonomy in fiscal matters while maintaining French banking infrastructure.

**Key Advantage:** Saint-Martin operates on territorial taxation (not worldwide), potentially allowing Bitcoin gains to be treated more favorably than in mainland France.

**Opportunity:** This is the only Caribbean jurisdiction where your second passport comes with EU mobility AND potential Bitcoin banking access.

**Risk Level:** Moderate (untested territory)  
**Upside:** First-mover advantage
